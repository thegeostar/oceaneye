【学生 1：水下机器人操作与数据采集】

Our project is called:
“OceanEye — AI Vision-driven Underwater Robot Intelligent Biological Detection
System.”

With the rapid development of artificial intelligence and marine technology, underwater
intelligent perception has become increasingly important in marine monitoring,
ecological protection, and underwater exploration.

However, underwater environments are very complex. Due to light absorption and
scattering, underwater images usually suffer from color distortion, low contrast, blurred
edges, and unclear biological targets.

Therefore, our project combines an underwater robot platform with artificial intelligence
technology to achieve intelligent underwater biological detection.

This is our underwater robot platform.

The robot integrates underwater cameras, motion control modules, communication
modules, and image acquisition systems.

During operation, the robot can move underwater and collect real-time visual information.

The complete workflow is:
Underwater robot image acquisition → AI image processing → Target detection →
Result analysis

Through this process, the system can automatically discover and recognize underwater
biological targets.

Next, my teammate will demonstrate how our AI algorithm processes underwater images
and performs target detection.


【学生 2：AI 视觉算法运行与目标检测】

Hello everyone.

I will introduce the artificial intelligence part of our system.
The main challenge of underwater biological detection is that targets usually have small
sizes, similar backgrounds, unclear boundaries, and complex underwater environments.

For example, sea cucumbers, sea urchins, scallops and starfish are often difficult to
identify.

To solve these problems, we use a deep learning-based object detection algorithm.

This is the original underwater image collected by the robot.

After AI processing, the system can automatically detect biological targets.

The detection results include target category, confidence score, and location information.

Our detection algorithm is based on an improved YOLOv8 model.

We introduce attention enhancement, global feature modeling and multi-scale feature
fusion technologies.

These improvements allow the system to achieve more accurate and stable detection
results in complex underwater environments.

When the underwater robot moves, the AI system can continuously analyze the video
stream and detect biological targets automatically.
