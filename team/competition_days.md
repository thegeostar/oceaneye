### 🔴 The 16:00–18:00 slot is the single most important two hours of the trip

Our whole strategy is that **25 of 100 points are rehearsal** (Skill Proficiency 15 + Operational Standardization 10). This is a rehearsal, on site, before it counts. Treat it as the real thing.

- **Bring the entire rig.** Tank, water, creatures, camera, torch, laptop, charger, USB sticks. Do not turn up to "just watch".
- **Find out what "drawing lots" decides** — almost certainly the running order within the track. Our slot time may be fixed here.
- **Test on the venue's projector.** The demo is built on a 1920px laptop screen. If the labels smear at the back of the room, the 15 Task Difficulty points are invisible and you have one evening to fix the font size.
- **Find the power sockets** and check the plug type. Measure how far your cable has to reach.
- **Ask whether water is permitted on stage**, and how long you get to set up before the clock starts.

Anything learned here goes straight into [`IF_IT_BREAKS.md`](IF_IT_BREAKS.md) the same evening.

## What "20 minutes" actually means

Straight from the invitation letter, *Participation Requirements*:

> "For teams participating in the on-site competition, they must complete the **skill operation within 20 minutes** at the competition venue, **followed by an on-site presentation** to demonstrate the team's professional skills, comprehensive quality, and innovation capabilities. All participating teams are required to bring their own competition-related equipment."

Read it carefully, because it is easy to get backwards:

- **The 20 minutes is the *operation* — the doing.** Setting up, running the camera, showing live detection, the turbidity ladder. It is a **ceiling, not a target**. Finishing early is fine; overrunning is not.
- **The presentation comes after, and the letter gives it no time limit.** That is an open question for the organisers — see [`../presentation/Open_Questions.md`](../presentation/Open_Questions.md).
- There is **no separate "20-minute presentation"**. That was our own misreading, corrected 16 September.

**Rough sanity check:** 3.5 hours for 15 groups across 3 parallel rooms ≈ 5 groups per room ≈ 40 minutes each, including changeover. So 20 minutes of operation plus a presentation of roughly 10–15 minutes fits. **This is arithmetic, not an official figure — confirm it.**
